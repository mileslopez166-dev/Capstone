Story Title: The Apple Tree

Story Description:
Mina found a small apple tree beside the school garden.
Every morning, she watered it before class.
One day, the tree gave its first bright red apple.

Question: What is an apple?

A. Fruit
B. Color
C. Thing
D. Person

Correct Answer: A

Question: Which word means the same as happy?

A. Sad
B. Glad
C. Angry
D. Tired

Correct Answer: B
