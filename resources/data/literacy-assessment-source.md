This file contains five original stories. Each story has eight Frustration-level support questions, eight Instructional-level practice questions, and eight Independent-level practice questions. The first story also retains its eight Advanced enrichment questions. There are 128 questions in total: 32 for Story 1 and 24 for each of Stories 2–5. Question numbering restarts for each story. The labels describe practice difficulty only; Advanced is enrichment, not an additional Phil-IRI or ML classification.

## Story 1

Story Title: A Clearer Book Fair
Story Description:
On Saturday, Liza’s Grade 6 class held a free book fair at their barangay hall. Each visitor could take one book home. Thirty donated books lay mixed on three tables, and visitors kept asking where to look. Ben suggested sorting by size. Liza chose stories, science, and poems instead. Classmates sorted the books and made cardboard signs. A younger visitor still needed help reading a sign, so Ben added drawings. Soon, visitors found books without help. The science table became empty before closing. Liza noted this in her notebook, saved the signs for next month, and thanked Ben for making them clearer.

Story Description:

Together, they made a weekly chart with names and spaces for completed tasks. Their teacher reminded them to check the soil before watering. On Friday, an absent pupil’s partner covered her turn. The plants grew stronger, and the class kept using the chart to share the work.

## Story 3

Story Title: The Changed Meeting Time
Story Description:
Mara reached her school’s covered court at two, ready for the storytelling club. Only Paolo was there. Their teacher had moved the meeting to three, but an old notice still showed two. Paolo wanted to write the new time over the old one. Mara worried that both times might remain visible. After checking with their teacher, they replaced the notice and wrote the date clearly. They also told classmates who had already read the earlier announcement. At three, the club gathered as planned. Before leaving, Mara asked who would remove outdated notices. Paolo volunteered and added the task to his notebook.
